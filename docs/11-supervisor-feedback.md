# 11. Supervisor Feedback

**Supervisor:** Kuralay Makhmetova

Throughout the work on the project, I regularly reviewed the team's materials and discussed the main decisions with them. I focused mainly on the problem statement, the realism of the scope of work, and how carefully the conclusions were worded.

The project is relevant: small farms often irrigate on a schedule or "by eye", and there are few accessible tools for them. The team clearly defined the goal, divided the work by priority and reasonably limited the scope of the first version. It is good that the documents openly state the project's limitations and make no promises of water savings or yield gains that have not yet been confirmed by anything.

The team made changes in response to my comments. I also advised wording the results of the model comparison more cautiously and not drawing conclusions broader than the data allow. In addition, I drew attention to the small size of the farmer survey. These comments were taken into account: the comparison with existing systems was reworked, the wording was softened, and verified findings and assumptions were separated in the documents.

At the next stage, the team needs to align the wording of the requirements with what is actually implemented and conduct additional conversations with farmers.

The work is proceeding in line with the pre-defense 1 plan, and my comments have led to noticeable improvements in the project.
