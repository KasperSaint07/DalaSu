## Description

The file contains five screens in a mobile and a web version: overview, add field, field details, irrigation recommendation and irrigation journal. They cover field management (FR-09), the irrigation recommendation (FR-05 to FR-08) and the irrigation journal (FR-10). Alerts (FR-11), season analytics (FR-12) and the KZ/RU language switch (FR-13) are not in the prototype. The screens are in Russian.

The recommendation screen shows the date and time window first, then the amount, and explains how the amount is obtained. This follows the survey: 7 of 8 respondents want the irrigation date and 5 of 8 want the amount ([02](02-evidence-of-relevance.md)).

All numbers in the prototype are demo values, and the screens are marked as a demo. The prototype does not call any model. No usability test with farmers has been done yet.
